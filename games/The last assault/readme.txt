Welcome to this game that doesn't have a title!

1: What is the game?
Right now, the game is basicly The Blood Rain in 3d. More will be added later to make this game different.
2: Controls
Arrows move.
Space jumps.
Number row selects weapons.
The control keys fire.
R reloads your weapon.
A says how much ammo you have for your weapon.
H says your health.
Tab and shift tab cycles through your quick inventory.
Enter without holding shift will interact with things.
Shift enter uses whatever item you are focused on in your quick inv.
I opens the inventory menu.
Escape goes back to the main menu.
3: Conclusion
One day this game will have a name, but right now, enjoy the game with no title!
